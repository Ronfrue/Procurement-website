About

Procurement that's transparent, efficient and fully compliant.

Fericka Frue
MBA · Procurement Specialist / Consultant
"Delivering transparent, efficient and compliant procurement solutions."

Fericka Frue is a procurement specialist and independent consultant with an MBA. She advises organizations end to end on building sourcing and tendering processes that are accountable from procurement planning through contract close-out — including proposal writing, bid evaluation, contract negotiation, and post-award compliance.

Her practice covers both public sector tendering and corporate procurement, with training and policy development offered alongside hands-on advisory work.
