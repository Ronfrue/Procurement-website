Privacy Notice

Last updated: July 26, 2026 · Applies to fericafrue.com and related consultation services
In short: This notice explains what personal data this website collects when you contact Fericka Frue or book a consultation, why it's collected, how it's protected, and the rights you have over it under Jamaica's Data Protection Act, 2020 (the "DPA").

1. Who This Notice Covers

This Privacy Notice is issued by Fericka Frue, MBA, operating as an independent procurement specialist and consultant ("Fericka Frue," "we," "us," or "our"), based in Jamaica. It applies to personal data collected through this website, the consultation request form, and the integrated scheduling tool (Calendly).

For the purposes of the DPA, Fericka Frue is the data controller in relation to personal data collected through this site — meaning she determines the purposes and means of processing that data.

2. Our Commitment to the Data Protection Act, 2020

Jamaica's Data Protection Act, 2020 governs how personal data is collected, used, stored, and shared by data controllers operating in or targeting individuals in Jamaica. The Act is enforced by the Office of the Information Commissioner (OIC), Jamaica's independent data protection supervisory authority.

This website's data practices are designed around the DPA's eight data protection standards, which require that personal data be:

  1. Processed fairly and lawfully;
  2. Obtained and used only for specified, lawful purposes;
  3. Adequate, relevant, and not excessive for those purposes (data minimisation);
  4. Accurate and, where necessary, kept up to date;
  5. Not retained for longer than necessary;
  6. Processed in accordance with the rights of data subjects under the Act;
  7. Protected by appropriate technical and organisational security measures; and
  8. Not transferred outside Jamaica unless the receiving country or recipient provides an adequate level of protection, or another safeguard recognised under the Act applies.

4. Legal Basis for Processing

We process your personal data on the following bases recognised under the DPA:

  • Consent — when you voluntarily submit the request form or book a consultation, you are consenting to us using that data to respond to you.
  • Legitimate interest — to operate a professional consulting practice and respond to business enquiries, in a manner that does not override your rights and freedoms as a data subject.
  • Contractual necessity — where processing is required to take steps toward, or perform, a consulting engagement you have requested.

5. How We Use and Share Your Data

Your data is used solely to respond to your enquiry, schedule and deliver consultations, and maintain reasonable business records. We do not sell personal data, and we do not use it for unrelated marketing without your separate consent.

We share limited personal data with the following third-party processors strictly to operate this website's functionality:

  • Formspree — processes and delivers consultation request form submissions.
  • Calendly — processes scheduling data to manage consultation bookings.

Cross-border transfers

Formspree and Calendly may process and store data on servers located outside Jamaica. Where personal data is transferred internationally in this way, we rely on the fact that these providers maintain recognised international security and privacy safeguards (including standard contractual protections and independently audited security programmes) as a basis for transfer, consistent with Standard 8 of the DPA governing cross-border data transfers.

6. Data Retention

We retain consultation enquiries and related correspondence only for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your enquiry, deliver any engagement you contract for, and meet legitimate business and legal record-keeping needs — after which it is deleted or anonymised.

7. Security Measures

We apply reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal data against unauthorised access, loss, misuse, or alteration, consistent with Standard 7 of the DPA. This includes restricting access to submitted enquiries and relying on reputable third-party processors with their own security safeguards. No online system is completely secure, and we encourage you not to send highly sensitive information through the contact form.

8. Your Rights as a Data Subject

Under the DPA, you have the right to:

  • Be informed of how your personal data is processed (this notice is part of that);
  • Access a copy of the personal data we hold about you;
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete personal data;
  • Request erasure of personal data that is no longer needed for the purpose it was collected;
  • Object to processing carried out on the basis of legitimate interest;
  • Not be subject to decisions based solely on automated processing that significantly affect you (we do not carry out any such automated decision-making); and
  • Lodge a complaint with the Office of the Information Commissioner if you believe your data protection rights have been violated.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in Section 10 below.

9. Changes to This Notice

We may update this Privacy Notice from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or in applicable law, including any amendments to the DPA or its Regulations. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.

10. Contact Us / Lodging a Complaint

If you have questions about this notice or wish to exercise your data protection rights, please contact us via the request form on the Contact section of this website.
